# Release notes draft 🚀

## Highlights ✨

- Throughput improved by a factor of ~2.3x 📈
- Error budget burn rate dropped below the alert line ✅
- New approximation: f(x) ≈ ∑ aₙxⁿ for |x| ≤ 1, with error ε < 10⁻⁶
- Geometry helpers now handle π, √2, ∞, and angles like 45° or θ = π/4
- Set operations: A ∪ B, A ∩ B, x ∈ S, and S ⊆ T are all supported

## Known issues 🐛

1. The ÷ and × glyphs render oversized at small font sizes 🔍
2. Currency cells mix € and ¥ when locales change mid-session 💱
3. Arrows in tooltips (→, ⇒, ↺) occasionally clip on narrow screens 📱

Thanks to everyone who filed reports this cycle 🙌 ❤️
